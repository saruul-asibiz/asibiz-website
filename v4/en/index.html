FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

How long does it take from request to arrival?

It depends on the role and the country, but usually about 4 to 6 months. People already living in Japan can sometimes start in 1 to 2 months.

How much Japanese do they speak?

Specified Skilled Workers have passed JLPT N4 or an equivalent test. For some roles we can introduce people with a higher level.

What about housing?

Housing is the employer’s role, but we help find and sign for a place and get everything ready for daily life.

What if someone quits early?

If someone we introduced leaves for their own reasons, we refund 90% of the placement fee within the first month, or 50% within 3 months.

We have never hired foreign staff. Can you help?

Yes. From the first idea, we go through visa status, preparation at your workplace and pre-arrival training for your Japanese staff, together.

When do we pay?

The placement fee is invoiced in the month the person arrives and starts. The support fee is invoiced monthly after that.

Message from our representative

Peace of mind for the people who work, and the companies that hire.

Asibiz began in 2017 in Ginowan, Okinawa. We started because we wanted to help two groups at once: companies short of hands, and young people across Asia who dream of working in Japan.

What matters most to us is not stopping at the introduction. With support in their own language and pre-arrival training for the workplace, we build places where people can work for a long time.
